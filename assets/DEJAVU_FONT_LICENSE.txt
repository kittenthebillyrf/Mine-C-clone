DejaVu Sans bitmap font resources
==================================

Files:
- dejavu.fnt

Font family: DejaVu Sans

License text and terms: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html

The bitmap atlas and BMFont descriptor are taken from the raylib examples
resource set (raysan5/raylib, examples/text/resources). The font descriptor
contains Unicode glyphs including Cyrillic, Latin, and other scripts.
